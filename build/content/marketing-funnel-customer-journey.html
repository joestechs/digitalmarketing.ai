The Marketing Funnel & Customer Journey

Every framework in digital marketing eventually maps back to one idea: strangers become customers through a series of predictable stages, and different tactics work at different stages.

The classic funnel: TOFU / MOFU / BOFU

StageCustomer mindsetGoalTypical tactics
TOFU (Top)"I have a problem"Get discoveredSEO blog posts, social content, YouTube, PR
MOFU (Middle)"Here are my options"Build trust, capture contact infoLead magnets, comparison content, email nurture, webinars
BOFU (Bottom)"I'm ready to buy"ConvertDemos, case studies, retargeting ads, free trials, discounts
Retention"Was this worth it?"Retain & expandOnboarding emails, loyalty programs, upsell campaigns, referrals

AIDA — the oldest copywriting funnel

AIDA (Attention, Interest, Desire, Action) predates the internet but still structures almost every landing page and ad you'll write:

  • Attention — a headline or visual that stops the scroll.
  • Interest — a hook that makes the problem feel relevant and urgent.
  • Desire — proof, benefits, and emotional payoff that makes the solution feel necessary.
  • Action — one clear, low-friction next step (a single CTA button, not five competing links).

The customer journey is not linear

In practice, people bounce between stages: someone reads a blog post (TOFU), leaves, sees a retargeting ad three days later (a BOFU tactic reaching a MOFU mindset), signs up for a newsletter (MOFU), and converts a month later after an email sequence. This is why multi-touch attribution (covered in Analytics) matters — a "last click" model would wrongly credit only the final ad.

🧪 Try It

Pick any product you bought online in the last month. Reconstruct your own journey: what first made you aware of it? What made you consider it seriously? What tipped you into buying? Map each touchpoint to TOFU/MOFU/BOFU — this exercise is a common take-home interview task.

🎯 Interview Corner

Q: A campaign gets tons of traffic but almost no conversions. What's your diagnosis process?
A: Work backwards through the funnel: check if traffic quality matches intent, check landing page relevance and load speed, check CTA clarity, then check whether there's a nurture path for people not ready to buy instead of losing them entirely.
