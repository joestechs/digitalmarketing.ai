Local SEO

Local SEO optimizes a business's visibility for geographically-bound searches — "near me" queries, map results, and local pack listings — critical for any business with a physical location or service area.

Google Business Profile (GBP)

The single highest-leverage local SEO asset. A complete, accurate, actively-managed Google Business Profile is required to appear in the "local pack" (the map + 3 listings shown above organic results for local queries).

  • Accurate business name, address, phone (NAP), category, and hours
  • Regular photo uploads and Google Posts (updates shown directly in the listing)
  • Prompt, professional responses to every review — both positive and negative
  • Accurate service area settings for businesses without a public storefront

NAP consistency

Your business Name, Address, and Phone number should be identical, character-for-character, across your website, GBP, and every directory (Yelp, Yellow Pages, industry-specific directories). Inconsistent NAP data confuses local ranking algorithms and erodes trust signals.

⚠ Common Mistake

Listing "St." on the website but "Street" on Yelp, or an old phone number lingering on a forgotten directory. Run a NAP audit with a tool like Moz Local or BrightLocal before investing further in local SEO.

Local citations & directories

A "citation" is any online mention of your NAP data, linked or not. Core citation sources include Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Maps, Yelp, and vertical-specific directories (e.g., Avvo for lawyers, Healthgrades for doctors).

On-page local signals

local-business-schema.json
<script type="application/ld+json">
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "LocalBusiness",
  "name": "Riverside Dental Care",
  "address": {
    "@type": "PostalAddress",
    "streetAddress": "123 Main St",
    "addressLocality": "Austin",
    "addressRegion": "TX",
    "postalCode": "78701"
  },
  "telephone": "+1-512-555-0100",
  "openingHours": "Mo-Fr 08:00-18:00"
}
</script>
  • A location page per service area, each with unique content (not a copy-pasted template with the city name swapped)
  • Embedded Google Map and clear NAP in the footer of every page
  • City/region names naturally worked into titles, headers, and body copy

Reviews as a ranking factor

Review count, average rating, recency, and keyword content within reviews all influence local pack rankings. A steady, ongoing flow of new reviews outperforms a one-time review push. Never buy fake reviews — it violates platform policy and risks profile suspension.

🎯 Interview Corner

Q: A local business ranks well organically but not in the local map pack. What would you check?
A: Google Business Profile completeness and category accuracy, NAP consistency across directories, review volume/recency compared to competitors in the pack, and proximity/relevance signals (is the GBP address and service area correctly configured for the searcher's location?).
