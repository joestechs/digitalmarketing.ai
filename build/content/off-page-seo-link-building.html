Off-Page SEO & Link Building

Off-page SEO covers everything that builds authority and trust outside your own site — primarily backlinks, but also brand mentions, reviews, and digital PR.

Why links still matter

A backlink is one site vouching for another. Google treats links as votes of confidence, weighted by the linking site's own authority and topical relevance. A single link from a respected, relevant publication can outweigh hundreds of low-quality directory links.

White-hat link building tactics

  • Digital PR / newsjacking — publish original data or studies journalists want to cite.
  • Guest posting — contribute genuinely useful articles to relevant industry sites.
  • Broken link building — find dead links on relevant pages and suggest your content as the replacement.
  • Skyscraper technique — find a well-linked piece of content, create something meaningfully better, then reach out to sites linking to the original.
  • Resource page outreach — get listed on curated "best tools/resources" pages in your niche.
  • HARO / journalist requests — respond to reporters seeking expert quotes in exchange for a citation link.
⚠ Black-Hat Tactics to Avoid

Buying links in bulk, private blog networks (PBNs), excessive reciprocal linking, and comment/forum spam all violate Google's spam policies and risk manual penalties that can remove a site from search results entirely. If a link-building tactic can be described as "getting a link without earning it," assume it carries risk.

Domain Authority vs. Domain Rating

DA (Moz) and DR (Ahrefs) are third-party metrics estimating a site's overall backlink strength on a 0–100 scale. Neither is used by Google directly — they're industry proxies for comparing sites, useful for prospecting link targets and benchmarking competitors.

Brand mentions & digital PR

Even unlinked brand mentions can contribute to how search engines perceive a brand's authority and trustworthiness in a topic space. Monitoring tools like Google Alerts or Mention.com help track and, where relevant, follow up to convert a mention into a link.

🎯 Interview Corner

Q: How would you build 10 quality backlinks for a new SaaS product with zero existing authority?
A: A strong answer names concrete tactics matched to a new site's constraints: create one genuinely useful free tool or original data study (a natural link magnet), pitch it to niche newsletters/blogs, get listed on relevant SaaS/startup directories, guest post on 2-3 adjacent-niche blogs, and respond to a handful of HARO/journalist requests in the space.
