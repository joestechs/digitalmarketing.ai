Video & YouTube Marketing

Video is the highest-engagement content format across nearly every platform, and YouTube is the world's second-largest search engine — with its own distinct ranking and discovery mechanics.

YouTube SEO basics

ElementWhy it matters
TitleInclude the target keyword naturally within the first ~60 characters
DescriptionFirst 2-3 lines show in search results; include keywords, links, and a summary
TagsMinor ranking factor now, but still help YouTube understand topic/context
ThumbnailThe single biggest lever on click-through rate — high contrast, readable at small size, expressive
Closed captions/transcriptImproves accessibility and gives YouTube's algorithm full text to index
ChaptersImproves watch experience and can surface in search results as jump-to timestamps

The metric YouTube cares about most

Audience retention (the percentage of a video actually watched) is the strongest signal YouTube's algorithm uses to decide whether to recommend a video further. A video with a high click-through rate but a steep early drop-off will get suppressed; a video that holds attention gets pushed to more suggested/home feeds.

💡 Tip

The first 15 seconds decide whether someone stays. Open with the payoff or a concrete preview of what's coming ("by the end of this video you'll be able to X") rather than a slow, generic intro.

Short-form vs. long-form video

Short-form (Reels/Shorts/TikTok)Long-form (YouTube videos)
Primary jobDiscovery, reach, top-of-funnelDepth, trust-building, monetization
Ideal length7-60 seconds8-20+ minutes for search-driven topics
DistributionAlgorithmic feed, low subscriber dependencySearch + subscriber notifications + suggested videos

Repurposing one video into a content system

  1. Record one long-form video (e.g., a tutorial or interview)
  2. Pull 3-5 short, high-energy clips for Reels/Shorts/TikTok
  3. Transcribe it → turn into a blog post with the video embedded (captures SEO search traffic too)
  4. Pull quotes for social graphics and an email newsletter section

🎯 Interview Corner

Q: What's the single most important metric for YouTube algorithm performance, and why?
A: Audience retention (average percentage viewed). It's the strongest proxy YouTube has for "is this actually satisfying," and it directly predicts how much a video gets recommended beyond people who already subscribed.
