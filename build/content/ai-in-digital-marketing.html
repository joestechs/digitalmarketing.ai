AI Overviews & the shifting SEO landscape

AI-generated answer summaries appearing directly in search results (and the rise of AI chat interfaces as a discovery channel) are changing how people find information — sometimes satisfying a query without a click to any website at all. This has given rise to Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) or AI SEO: writing content structured to be clearly extractable and citable by AI systems, alongside traditional SEO — clear headings, direct answers, and well-cited factual claims.

💡 Practical GEO tips

Answer the core question directly and early in a section (AI systems favor extractable, self-contained answers). Use structured data. Build genuine topical authority and citations — the same E-E-A-T signals that help traditional SEO also help AI systems trust and surface your content.

Using AI responsibly in a marketing workflow

  • Fact-check AI output — language models can produce confident, plausible-sounding errors ("hallucinations"); never publish AI claims/statistics unverified.
  • Disclose where required — some platforms and jurisdictions require disclosure of AI-generated content, particularly for synthetic media/deepfakes.
  • Protect customer data — avoid pasting personally identifiable customer information into public AI tools without appropriate data-processing agreements.
  • Keep a human editorial pass — AI-drafted content that goes out completely unedited tends to read generically and can hurt brand trust and even SEO quality signals.

A simple AI-assisted content workflow

  1. Human does keyword/audience research and outlines the argument/structure
  2. AI drafts a first pass from that outline (faster than a blank page)
  3. Human rewrites for voice, adds real examples/data/opinions AI can't originate
  4. Human fact-checks every claim and statistic before publishing
🎯 Interview Corner

Q: How has AI changed your approach to content marketing?
A: A strong answer avoids both extremes ("AI writes everything now" or "I don't use it at all"). Frame it as a workflow shift: AI accelerates research and first drafts, freeing more time for strategy, original insight, and the fact-checking/editing that actually determines content quality and trust.
