PPC & Google Ads Fundamentals

Pay-Per-Click advertising lets you buy placement at the top of search results (or across the web) and pay only when someone clicks — turning marketing spend into an auction you can optimize in near real time.

Add negative keywords (e.g. -free, -jobs) to exclude irrelevant searches and stop wasting budget on clicks that will never convert.

Quality Score

Google scores every keyword 1–10 based on three components. It doesn't directly set your rank, but it heavily influences your effective cost-per-click and ad rank in the auction — a higher Quality Score means you can pay less to achieve the same position.

  • Expected CTR — historical likelihood your ad gets clicked for that keyword
  • Ad relevance — how closely the ad text matches the keyword's intent
  • Landing page experience — relevance, load speed, and usability of the destination page

Ad Rank formula (conceptually)

ad-rank.txt
Ad Rank = Max CPC bid × Quality Score × expected impact of ad extensions/format

// A lower bid with a higher Quality Score can outrank
// a higher bid with poor relevance and a weak landing page.

Bidding strategies

StrategyOptimizes forGood for
Manual CPCYou set max bid per clickFull control, small/testing accounts
Maximize ClicksMost clicks within budgetEarly traffic building
Target CPAAutomated bidding toward a cost-per-acquisition goalLead gen with conversion tracking set up
Target ROASAutomated bidding toward a return-on-ad-spend goalE-commerce with revenue tracking
💡 Tip

Automated bidding strategies (Target CPA/ROAS) need enough conversion volume (Google recommends 15-30+ conversions in the last 30 days) to learn effectively. Start new campaigns with manual or Maximize Clicks bidding, then switch once you have data.

🎯 Interview Corner

Q: A campaign has a high CTR but a low conversion rate. What's wrong?
A: The ad copy is doing its job (people click), so the mismatch is downstream — usually a landing page that doesn't match ad expectations, a confusing or slow checkout/form, or the keyword traffic being lower-intent than the ad promised. Always check ad-to-landing-page message match first.
