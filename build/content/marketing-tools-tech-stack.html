💡 Building a stack on a $0 budget

Google Search Console + GA4 + Google Ads (Keyword Planner) + Canva Free + Mailchimp Free + Meta Business Suite covers analytics, SEO research, design, email, and social scheduling — a genuinely capable starter stack at zero cost.

🎯 Interview Corner

Q: What tools have you used, and how do you decide when a paid tool is worth it over a free alternative?
A: A strong answer ties tool choice to a specific need/limitation hit with the free option (e.g., "Search Console only shows my own site's data; I needed Ahrefs to analyze competitor backlink gaps") rather than listing tool names without justification.
