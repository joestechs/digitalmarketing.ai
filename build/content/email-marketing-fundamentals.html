Email & Automation

Email Marketing Fundamentals

Email consistently delivers the highest ROI of any digital marketing channel because you own the audience — no algorithm decides whether your message gets shown.

Core email types

TypePurpose
NewsletterRegular value-driven content to stay top-of-mind
Welcome seriesAutomated sequence triggered by sign-up; sets expectations, builds trust
PromotionalSales, launches, limited-time offers
TransactionalOrder confirmations, receipts, password resets — triggered by user actions
Re-engagement / win-backTargets inactive subscribers before removing them from the list

Key metrics & healthy benchmarks

MetricFormulaRough healthy range*
Open rateOpens ÷ Delivered × 10015–30%
Click-through rate (CTR)Clicks ÷ Delivered × 1002–5%
Click-to-open rate (CTOR)Clicks ÷ Opens × 10010–20%
Unsubscribe rateUnsubscribes ÷ Delivered × 100< 0.5%
Bounce rateBounces ÷ Sent × 100< 2%

*Varies significantly by industry and list quality — use as a rough sanity check, not a hard target.

⚠ Common Mistake

Using modern CSS (flexbox, grid) in email HTML. Many email clients (Outlook especially) use outdated rendering engines — table-based layouts with inline styles remain the safest cross-client approach.

Deliverability basics

  • SPF, DKIM, DMARC — DNS records that authenticate your domain as a legitimate sender, reducing spam-folder placement.
  • List hygiene — regularly remove hard bounces and chronically inactive subscribers; a "clean" engaged list improves deliverability for everyone else on it.
  • Double opt-in — requiring email confirmation before adding someone to a list improves list quality and legal compliance.
  • Avoid spam-trigger patterns — ALL CAPS SUBJECT LINES, excessive exclamation points, and known spam phrases hurt inbox placement.

Segmentation basics

Sending the same email to everyone underperforms sending relevant emails to smaller groups. Common segments: signup source, engagement level (active/inactive), purchase history, funnel stage, declared interests from a preference center.

🎯 Interview Corner

Q: Open rates dropped sharply after Apple's Mail Privacy Protection rollout. How do you adjust measurement?
A: Open rate became unreliable as a primary metric once Apple began pre-fetching images for privacy, inflating opens artificially. Shift primary optimization to click-through rate and downstream conversions, and use open rate only as a rough directional signal, not an absolute number.
