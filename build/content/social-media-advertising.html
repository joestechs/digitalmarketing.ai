Social Media Advertising

Social ads target people based on who they are and what they're interested in — rather than what they're actively searching for — making them powerful for awareness, discovery, and highly specific audience targeting.

Search ads vs. social ads

Search (Google Ads)Social (Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn)
IntentHigh — user is actively searchingLower — user is browsing/scrolling
Targeting basisKeywords typedDemographics, interests, behaviors, lookalikes
Best forBottom-of-funnel, transactional intentAwareness, discovery, visually-driven products
FormatMostly textImage, video, carousel, native-feeling content

Platform-by-platform ad strengths

PlatformStrongest forKey ad format
Meta (Facebook/Instagram)Broad reach, e-commerce, detailed interest targetingAdvantage+ campaigns, Reels ads
TikTok AdsGen Z/Millennial reach, viral-style native creativeIn-feed video, Spark Ads (boosting organic posts)

Audience targeting types

  • Core/Interest targeting — demographics + declared interests/behaviors.
  • Custom Audiences — built from your own data: website visitors (via pixel), email lists, app users.
  • Lookalike / Advantage Audiences — the platform finds new people who resemble your best existing customers.
  • Retargeting audiences — people who visited your site or engaged with content but didn't convert.

Creative best practices

  • Design for sound-off viewing — use captions; most social video is watched muted.
  • Hook within the first 1-2 seconds of video; feeds punish slow openings.
  • Native-feeling creative (looks like organic content) usually outperforms polished, obviously "ad-like" creative.
  • Always run 3-5 creative variants per ad set — creative fatigue sets in within days on high-frequency platforms.
🎯 Interview Corner

Q: How would you decide budget split between Google Search Ads and Meta Ads for a new e-commerce brand?
A: Consider funnel stage and existing demand: if the product solves a problem people already search for, weight Search Ads higher (capturing existing intent, generally lower CPA). If it's a novel/impulse product with little existing search volume, weight social ads higher to build awareness and discovery first, then layer in search/retargeting.
