Glossary A–Z

Every acronym and term used across this guide, in one searchable place. Use the search bar in the top nav, or jump by letter below.

A/B Testing
Comparing two variants of a page, email, or ad to measure which performs better against a defined metric.
AIDA
Attention, Interest, Desire, Action — a classic copywriting funnel framework.
AOV (Average Order Value)
Total revenue divided by number of orders.
Attribution Model
The rule set used to assign conversion credit across multiple marketing touchpoints (e.g. last-click, linear, data-driven).
Backlink
A link from one website to another; a core off-page SEO ranking signal.
Bounce Rate
The percentage of visitors who leave after viewing only one page without further interaction.
BOFU (Bottom of Funnel)
The decision stage of the customer journey, closest to purchase.
CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)
Total sales & marketing spend divided by number of new customers acquired.
Canonical Tag
An HTML tag (rel="canonical") specifying the preferred version of a page to prevent duplicate content issues.
CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift)
A Core Web Vital measuring visual stability during page load.
CPA (Cost Per Acquisition)
Total spend divided by number of conversions.
CPC (Cost Per Click)
Total spend divided by number of clicks.
CPM (Cost Per Mille)
Cost per 1,000 ad impressions.
CRO (Conversion Rate Optimization)
The systematic process of increasing the percentage of visitors who complete a desired action.
CTR (Click-Through Rate)
Clicks divided by impressions, expressed as a percentage.
DA / DR (Domain Authority / Domain Rating)
Third-party (Moz / Ahrefs) estimates of a domain's overall backlink strength, on a 0-100 scale.
DKIM / SPF / DMARC
Email authentication DNS records that verify sender legitimacy and improve deliverability.
DSP (Demand-Side Platform)
A platform advertisers use to buy programmatic ad inventory.
E-E-A-T
Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness — Google's content quality framework.
Engagement Rate
Total interactions (likes, comments, shares) divided by followers or reach.
Featured Snippet
A direct-answer box shown above normal Google search results ("position zero").
Funnel
The staged model (TOFU/MOFU/BOFU) describing how strangers become customers.
GA4 (Google Analytics 4)
Google's event-based web/app analytics platform.
GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)
Optimizing content to be extractable and citable by AI answer engines, alongside traditional SEO.
GTM (Google Tag Manager)
A tag management system for deploying tracking scripts without editing site code directly.
Impression
One instance of an ad or piece of content being displayed to a user.
Inbound Marketing
Earning attention through useful, relevant content rather than interrupting with outbound advertising.
INP (Interaction to Next Paint)
A Core Web Vital measuring page responsiveness to user input.
Keyword Difficulty (KD)
An estimated score of how hard it is to rank on page one for a given keyword.
KPI (Key Performance Indicator)
A metric explicitly tied to a business objective, used to judge success.
Lead Magnet
A free resource offered in exchange for a visitor's contact information.
Lead Scoring
Assigning points to leads based on fit and behavior to prioritize sales follow-up.
LCP (Largest Contentful Paint)
A Core Web Vital measuring the load time of a page's main content.
LTV / CLV (Customer Lifetime Value)
The total value a customer generates over their entire relationship with a business.
MOFU (Middle of Funnel)
The consideration stage of the customer journey.
Marketing Automation
Software that triggers marketing messages based on user behavior without manual sending.
NAP (Name, Address, Phone)
Core business identity data that must stay consistent across the web for local SEO.
Negative Keyword
A keyword explicitly excluded from triggering a PPC ad.
NPS (Net Promoter Score)
A loyalty metric: % Promoters minus % Detractors.
On-Page SEO
Optimization of elements directly on a webpage (titles, headers, content) for search visibility.
Organic Traffic
Visitors arriving via unpaid search engine results.
Outbound Marketing
Marketing that interrupts an audience's attention (cold ads, cold emails) rather than earning it.
Programmatic Advertising
Automated, real-time-bidding-based buying of digital ad inventory.
PPC (Pay-Per-Click)
An advertising model where the advertiser pays only when their ad is clicked.
Persona
A semi-fictional profile of an ideal customer, built from real research.
Quality Score
Google Ads' 1-10 estimate of keyword/ad relevance, influencing cost and ad rank.
Retargeting / Remarketing
Showing ads specifically to people who previously visited your site or engaged with your content.
ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)
Revenue divided by ad spend.
ROI (Return on Investment)
(Revenue − Cost) ÷ Cost × 100 — net profitability including all costs.
RTB (Real-Time Bidding)
The automated auction process behind programmatic ad buying.
Schema Markup / Structured Data
JSON-LD or microdata that explicitly tells search engines what type of content a page contains.
SEM (Search Engine Marketing)
Broadly, all search marketing; commonly used to mean paid search specifically.
SEO (Search Engine Optimization)
Improving a site to earn free organic rankings in search engines.
SERP (Search Engine Results Page)
The page of results returned for a search query.
SMART Goal
Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound goal-setting framework.
SSP (Supply-Side Platform)
A platform publishers use to sell ad inventory programmatically.
TOFU (Top of Funnel)
The awareness stage of the customer journey.
Topical Authority
The degree to which a site is trusted as a comprehensive, credible source on a subject.
UTM Parameters
URL tags (source, medium, campaign, content, term) used to track traffic origin in analytics tools.
Vanity Metric
A metric that looks impressive but doesn't reliably predict or explain business outcomes.
YMYL (Your Money or Your Life)
Google's classification for content topics (health, finance, safety) held to a higher quality/trust bar.
