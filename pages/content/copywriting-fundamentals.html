Copywriting Fundamentals

Copywriting is writing with a specific job: get someone to take one action. Every headline, CTA button, and product description in digital marketing is copywriting.

Features vs. benefits

A feature is what something is. A benefit is what it does for the reader. Copy that lists features reads like a spec sheet; copy that leads with benefits reads like a solution.

FeatureBenefit
"256-bit encryption""Your customers' data stays safe, even if a laptop is stolen"
"24/7 live chat support""Get unstuck at 2am without waiting for a callback"
"Unlimited storage""Never delete an old project to make room for a new one"

Headline formulas that consistently work

  • How to [achieve desirable outcome] without [common pain point] — "How to Rank #1 Without Buying a Single Link"
  • [Number] Ways to [outcome] — "7 Ways to Cut Your Customer Acquisition Cost in Half"
  • The [Adjective] Guide to [Topic] — "The No-Nonsense Guide to Email Deliverability"
  • [Do X] Like [Authority/Group] — "Write Subject Lines Like a Top 1% Newsletter"

Writing calls-to-action (CTAs)

  • Use active, first-person or command verbs: "Start My Free Trial" often outperforms generic "Submit."
  • Make the value explicit, not just the action: "Get My Free Audit" beats "Download."
  • One CTA per screen/section — competing CTAs split attention and lower overall conversion.
  • Address the objection right next to the button ("No credit card required") to remove last-second hesitation.

Tone & voice

Voice is your brand's consistent personality (playful, authoritative, minimalist); tone is how that voice adapts to context (an error message vs. a celebration email use the same voice, different tones). Document both in a lightweight style guide so copy stays consistent across a team.

💡 Tip

Read your copy out loud. Sentences that are awkward to say are usually awkward to read. Cut any sentence that doesn't survive being read aloud without stumbling.

Editing checklist

  • Cut every adjective/adverb that doesn't change meaning ("very," "really," "amazing")
  • Replace passive voice with active voice where possible
  • Shorten sentences over ~25 words
  • Confirm the one core message survives if a reader only reads the headline and CTA
🎯 Interview Corner

Q: Rewrite this feature-led line as benefit-led: "Our app has real-time sync across devices."
A: A strong sample answer: "Pick up exactly where you left off — on any device, instantly." Interviewers are checking whether you can translate technical specs into reader-facing value on the spot.
