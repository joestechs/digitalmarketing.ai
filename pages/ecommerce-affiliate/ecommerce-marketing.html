E-commerce & Affiliate

E-commerce Marketing

E-commerce marketing applies every channel in this guide toward one specific, measurable goal: getting someone to complete a purchase online — with a few channel-specific tactics unique to online retail.

Product page optimization

  • High-quality images from multiple angles, with zoom — the closest online substitute for in-person inspection.
  • Benefit-driven descriptions, not just spec lists (tie back to Copywriting Fundamentals).
  • Reviews & ratings visible above the fold — social proof directly reduces purchase hesitation.
  • Clear shipping/returns info near the buy button — a top reason for cart abandonment is uncertainty about these.
  • Structured data (Product schema) so search results can show price/rating rich snippets.
product-schema.json
<script type="application/ld+json">
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "Product",
  "name": "Wide-Fit Trail Running Shoe",
  "image": "https://example.com/shoe.jpg",
  "offers": { "@type": "Offer", "price": "89.99", "priceCurrency": "USD", "availability": "https://schema.org/InStock" },
  "aggregateRating": { "@type": "AggregateRating", "ratingValue": "4.8", "reviewCount": "1204" }
}
</script>

Cart & checkout optimization

Common friction pointFix
Forced account creationOffer guest checkout
Unexpected shipping costs at checkoutShow estimated total cost earlier in the flow
Too many form fieldsUse address autocomplete, minimize required fields
Limited payment optionsSupport major cards + at least one digital wallet (Apple Pay/Google Pay)
No trust signalsSecurity badges, clear return policy link near payment fields

Marketplace SEO (Amazon, Etsy)

Marketplace search algorithms (e.g. Amazon's A9/A10) weigh different signals than Google: conversion rate, sales velocity, keyword-rich titles/bullet points, and review quantity/recency matter heavily. Optimizing a marketplace listing is closer to CRO than traditional web SEO — the ranking system directly rewards what sells.

E-commerce email flows

FlowTypical trigger
Abandoned cartItem added to cart, no purchase within X hours
Browse abandonmentViewed product(s), didn't add to cart
Post-purchaseOrder confirmation → shipping updates → review request → replenishment reminder
Win-backNo purchase in 60-90+ days for a previously active customer

Key e-commerce metrics

Beyond the general formulas covered in Marketing Metrics: Cart Abandonment Rate (carts started ÷ carts not completed), Repeat Purchase Rate, and Revenue Per Visitor (RPV) = Total Revenue ÷ Total Sessions — a single number that blends traffic quality and conversion performance.

🎯 Interview Corner

Q: Cart abandonment is at 75%. Walk me through how you'd reduce it.
A: 75% is actually near industry average, so first benchmark against the specific vertical rather than assuming crisis. Then diagnose with session recordings and checkout funnel analytics to find the exact drop-off step, fix the top friction point identified (often shipping cost surprise or forced account creation), and layer in an abandoned-cart email sequence as a safety net.
