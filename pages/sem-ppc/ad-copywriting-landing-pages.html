SEM & Paid Ads

Ad Copywriting & Landing Pages

A great ad earns the click. A great landing page earns the conversion. They have to say the same thing, or you pay for traffic that bounces.

Anatomy of a Google Responsive Search Ad

rsa-example.txt
Headline 1: Wide-Fit Trail Shoes
Headline 2: Free Returns, 30-Day Trial
Headline 3: Rated 4.8/5 by 12,000+ Runners
Description 1: Engineered for wide feet without sacrificing trail grip. Shop the collection.
Description 2: Free shipping over $50. See why runners switched.
Final URL: https://example.com/wide-fit-trail-shoes
Display Path: /wide-fit/trail

Google mixes and tests combinations of up to 15 headlines and 4 descriptions automatically — provide varied angles (feature, benefit, proof, urgency) rather than near-duplicate phrasing so the algorithm has real options to test.

Copywriting formulas

FormulaStructure
PASProblem → Agitate → Solution
AIDAAttention → Interest → Desire → Action
FABFeature → Advantage → Benefit
4UsUseful, Urgent, Unique, Ultra-specific (for headlines)

Landing page conversion checklist

  • Message match — the headline mirrors the ad's promise word-for-word where possible.
  • One goal, one CTA — remove nav links and distractions that let visitors leave before converting.
  • Above-the-fold clarity — a visitor should understand the offer within 5 seconds without scrolling.
  • Social proof — reviews, logos, testimonials, or numbers ("12,000+ customers") near the CTA.
  • Fast load time — every extra second of load time measurably drops conversion rate.
  • Mobile-optimized form — minimize required fields; every extra field lowers completion rate.
landing-page.html
<section class="hero">
  <h1>Wide-Fit Trail Shoes That Don't Compromise on Grip</h1>
  <p>Engineered toe box. Rated 4.8/5 by 12,000+ runners. Free 30-day trial.</p>
  <a class="cta" href="/shop" onclick="gtag('event','cta_click',{ location:'hero' })">
    Shop the Collection
  </a>
</section>
⚠ Common Mistake

Sending every ad to the homepage. Dedicated landing pages that match each ad's specific promise consistently outperform generic homepages — Quality Score and conversion rate both improve.

A/B testing ad copy

Run at least two headline/description variants per ad group and let each accumulate statistically meaningful impressions (typically 1,000+ per variant) before declaring a winner. Test one variable at a time when possible — swapping both the headline and the CTA at once makes it impossible to know which change moved the needle.

🎯 Interview Corner

Q: Walk me through how you'd write ad copy for a product you know nothing about.
A: Start from the persona and search intent, not the product: what problem is the searcher trying to solve right now? Pull 2-3 real objections or desires from reviews/customer language, map them to a formula like PAS, then write 5+ headline variants testing different angles (price, speed, trust, uniqueness) rather than one "perfect" line.
