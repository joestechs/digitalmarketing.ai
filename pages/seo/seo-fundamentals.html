SEO Fundamentals

Search Engine Optimization is the practice of shaping a website so search engines can find it, understand it, and rank it above competitors for the queries your audience actually types.

How a search engine actually works

  1. Crawling — bots ("spiders") like Googlebot follow links across the web to discover pages.
  2. Indexing — the content of discovered pages is analyzed and stored in a massive searchable database.
  3. Ranking — when someone searches, an algorithm scores every relevant indexed page against 200+ signals and returns an ordered list.

SEO work maps directly onto these three stages: technical SEO makes crawling/indexing possible, on-page SEO improves what's understood, and off-page SEO plus content quality improve ranking.

The three pillars of SEO

PillarQuestion it answersCovered in
Technical SEOCan search engines crawl and render this site efficiently?Technical SEO page
On-Page SEODoes this specific page clearly signal what it's about?On-Page SEO page
Off-Page SEODo other trustworthy sites vouch for this content?Off-Page SEO page

Search intent — the concept everything else depends on

Google's job is to match a query to the content type the searcher actually wants. Every keyword carries one of four intents:

IntentExample queryContent that ranks
Informational"what is domain authority"Blog post, guide, explainer
Navigational"ahrefs login"The brand's own page
Commercial investigation"best seo tools 2026"Comparison / listicle
Transactional"buy ahrefs subscription"Pricing / product page
⚠ Common Mistake

Writing a product page and trying to rank it for an informational keyword (or vice versa). Google has already decided, via the current top 10 results, what content type it believes satisfies that query — check the SERP before you write a word.

E-E-A-T

Google's quality raters evaluate content on Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness. It's not a direct ranking factor you can "add," but it explains why author bios, citations, first-hand case studies, and secure/transparent sites tend to outrank thin, anonymous content — especially for "Your Money or Your Life" (YMYL) topics like health and finance.

🎯 Interview Corner

Q: What's the difference between SEO and SEM?
A: SEO is earning organic (unpaid) rankings through content and technical optimization. SEM traditionally means the entire search marketing discipline, but in practice most people use it to mean paid search (PPC ads on search engines). SEO compounds slowly and is "free" per click; SEM/PPC is instant but you pay per click.
