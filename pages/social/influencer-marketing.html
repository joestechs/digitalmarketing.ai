Influencer Marketing

Influencer marketing borrows the trust a creator has already built with their audience — trading payment, product, or commission for authentic-feeling promotion.

Influencer tiers

TierFollower rangeStrength
Nano1K–10KHighest engagement rate, most authentic, lowest cost — often product-gift only
Micro10K–100KStrong niche trust, good ROI, still affordable
Macro100K–1MBroader reach, more polished, higher cost
Mega/Celebrity1M+Mass awareness, lowest relative engagement rate, highest cost
💡 Tip

A campaign of 15 well-matched micro/nano creators frequently outperforms one macro-influencer post at similar total cost — you get more authentic content variety and audiences that trust the recommendation more.

Vetting a creator before reaching out

  • Audience-brand fit — does their audience's demographics and interests actually match your persona?
  • Engagement rate, not just follower count — check for suspiciously round follower growth spikes (a sign of bought followers).
  • Content quality & brand safety — review recent posts for tone/values alignment.
  • Past brand partnerships — do sponsored posts get real engagement, or visibly less than organic posts?

Common deal structures

StructureHow it works
Flat feeFixed payment per post/video regardless of performance
Affiliate/commissionCreator earns a % of sales via a unique tracked link or code
Gifted productFree product in exchange for a post — common with nano creators
HybridSmaller flat fee + commission — aligns incentive with performance
Whitelisting / Ad partnershipBrand runs paid ads through the creator's handle for extra trust + targeting

Disclosure & compliance

Sponsored content must be clearly disclosed (e.g. #ad, platform-native "Paid Partnership" tags) under regulations like the FTC guidelines in the US. This isn't optional — undisclosed sponsorships risk platform penalties and regulatory action against both creator and brand.

Measuring influencer campaigns

  • Unique promo codes or UTM-tagged links per creator to attribute sales directly
  • Engagement rate and reach relative to the creator's typical performance (ask for past post insights before the campaign)
  • Brand lift / sentiment in comments, not just click metrics
🎯 Interview Corner

Q: How would you evaluate whether an influencer partnership was successful if the product isn't sold directly online?
A: Shift measurement from direct-response metrics to brand metrics: track branded search volume before/after, use unique promo codes redeemable in-store, survey new customers on how they heard about the brand, and monitor engagement quality/sentiment in the post's comments.
